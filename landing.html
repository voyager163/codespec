Features

Everything you need,
nothing you don't

A complete platform for AI-assisted Power Apps development — with humans firmly in control.

📋

Your Goal Drives Everything

Write what you want to build in plain language. PowerCodex reads it, plans the work, and remembers every decision — so nothing gets lost and you can always see why choices were made.

🔄

Runs the Hard Steps For You

PowerCodex automatically moves through every step — reading your goal, planning the work, building the app, opening it, and checking that it works — without you needing to do any of it by hand.

🩹

Fixes Itself When Something Breaks

If something stops working, PowerCodex figures out what went wrong, plans a fix, and rebuilds the app automatically. You get a working app, not an error message to decode.

⏸

Nothing Happens Without Your OK

PowerCodex always pauses before making changes to your Power Platform environment. You review the plan and say yes — or change direction. It never acts on your behalf without asking first.

📊

A Dashboard That Tells You What's Happening

A simple status screen shows exactly what PowerCodex is doing right now — which step it's on, what passed, and what needs your attention. No technical knowledge needed to read it.

🔒

Safe and Secure by Default

PowerCodex sets up industry-standard safety checks automatically. Your app is protected from day one — you don't need to know what those checks are or how to set them up.

Lifecycle

Seven stages.
One continuous loop.

The lifecycle engine runs autonomously between consent gates, so you review decisions — not minutiae.

1
Read Goal

Tell it what you want to build

Write your goal in plain English — like a note to a colleague. No forms to fill out, no technical setup. PowerCodex reads it and gets to work.

2
Make a Plan

PowerCodex figures out the steps

It breaks your goal into a clear list of tasks and writes down exactly what it plans to do. You can read the plan before anything starts — in plain language, not code.

3
Approve Your moment to decide

You say yes before anything changes

PowerCodex stops and waits for you here. Review the plan, ask questions, change direction — nothing happens to your app until you give the go-ahead.

4
Build

PowerCodex builds the app

It creates your Power App automatically, following the plan you approved. You can watch the progress on the dashboard while it works — no action needed from you.

5
Open

Your app goes live in Power Platform

PowerCodex publishes your app to your Power Platform environment and opens it automatically. You get a link so you can open it straight away.

6
Test

It checks the app works properly

PowerCodex uses the app the same way a real user would — clicking through screens, filling in forms, checking results. If anything doesn't work as expected, it flags it immediately.

7
Fix if Needed

Problems get fixed automatically

If the tests find an issue, PowerCodex figures out what went wrong, plans a fix, and rebuilds. If everything passes, it's done — and waits for your next goal.
